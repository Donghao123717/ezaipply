# Hashtag Guide: #collegeapps / #collegeapplication

## Community Vibe
A mix of high school juniors and seniors sharing updates, frustrations, and wins during
the college application cycle. Content ranges from emotional ("I got in!") to informational
("how to write a common app essay"). Supportive and relatable content performs best.

## Engagement Tips
- Acknowledge the shared struggle before jumping into advice
- Use relatable language: "I remember feeling exactly like this..."
- Short, punchy comments get more replies than long walls of text
- Ask a follow-up question to encourage a response

## Common Post Types to Engage
- "Finally submitted all my apps! 🎉" — celebrate with them
- "My Common App essay is due tomorrow and I haven't started 😭" — offer quick advice
- "Just got waitlisted at my dream school..." — empathize + constructive next steps
- "For anyone starting their college apps, here's what helped me" — engage positively
- "Is EZCollegeApp / [any tool] worth it for college apps?" — share personal experience naturally

## Tone Adjustment
More visual and emoji-friendly than Reddit. Shorter sentences. Personal and relatable storytelling
works much better than bullet-point advice. Lead with empathy, follow with a practical tip.
